  • arxiv-cs-ai：前沿 AI 方向信号 前沿 AI

    Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly explored for industrial tasks, yet few benchmarks evaluate their mastery of industrial knowledge across different types of cognitive demand. We introduce IndustrialKnowEval (IndKnow), a benchmark for industrial knowledge and engineering question answering. It contains 43,282 samples covering 21 task types across seven industrial sectors, including aerospace, electric power, and metallurgy, organized into four levels of cognitive demand: memory, comprehension, reasoning, and engineering application. Enterprise-based domain specialists and experienced researchers participate in all stages of dataset construction and quality control. We conduct a series of experiments on 18 representative LLMs, with domain experts deeply involved in the evaluation. Building upon IndKnow, we propose the Industrial Cognitive Capability Gap (ICCG) metric to characterize the performance differential between foundational industrial knowledge and higher-order reasoning and application, and we analyze it alongside absolute performance to provide a more complete view of model capabilities. We anticipate that IndustrialKnowEval will support the benchmarking of LLMs in industrial scenarios and promote the development of robust industrial intelligence. Code is available at: https://github.com/J4cksonLee/IndKnow
